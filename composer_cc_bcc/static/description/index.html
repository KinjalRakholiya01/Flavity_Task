Composer Cc & Bcc

Cc and Bcc in the mail composer of sales orders, quotations and CRM leads

Cc: visible copy, shown in the Cc header to every recipient.
Bcc: blind copy, delivered through the SMTP envelope only; no header of any received email mentions it.

Workflow

  1. Open "Send by Email" on a quotation / order, or the full composer on a lead.
  2. Fill To, and add contacts in Cc and/or Bcc.
  3. Send: one email goes out with To + Cc headers; Bcc contacts receive it invisibly.
  4. Check what was sent in Settings > Technical > Email > Emails (Cc and Bcc fields).
